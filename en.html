The Band

Between the Atacama Desert and the Spree

Uru Pampa was born in the streets of Iquique, Chile – a land of salt, eternal sun, and music that tastes like roots. Carrying their charango, quena, and guitar, they traveled all the way to Berlin.

In the German capital, they found a second home: a vibrant scene that invites them to share Andean rhythms, Cumbia, and Cueca with the world.
